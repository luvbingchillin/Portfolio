Each project is curated as an accessioned library monograph with custom leather tones, Library of Congress call numbers, and procedural gilded bindings.

- Critical structural finding: Missing Tailwind utilities in `src/index.css` (`md:grid-cols-2`, `divide-y`, `md:divide-x`) force the two-page archival monograph spread into a single vertical column on desktop. Missing `.sm:inline` makes desktop header text invisible.

#### Overall Impression
A breathtaking, museum-grade 3D library that stands out in the top 1% of developer portfolios. However, interaction ambiguities, missing touchscreen handlers, and missing CSS grid utilities create sharp friction that undermines its potential.

#### What's Working
1. **Theatrical Arc & Kinetic Inertia:** The 4-beat mechanical odometer preloader and 1.15s cylindrical fan reveal establish an authentic physical presence.
2. **Harmonious Optical Tracking:** Display titles (-0.04em), body copy (-0.025em), and metadata (0.20em) create a disciplined editorial rhythm.
3. **Tactile Craft Details:** Procedural leather textures, gold foil accents, and crimson velvet bookmark ribbons ground the digital experience in historical bookmaking.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P0] Missing Touch & Pointer Handlers in 3D Stage**: Container only listens to mouse events (`mousedown`, `mousemove`). Touchscreen users on phones and tablets cannot swipe or rotate books.
- **[P1] Missing CSS Grid Utilities for Two-Page Spread**: `md:grid-cols-2` is missing from `src/index.css`, collapsing the archival two-page spread into a single vertical column on desktop.
- **[P1] Interaction Affordance Void**: No on-screen cue indicates that dragging spins the book or clicking opens the monograph.
- **[P1] Mobile Layout Collision**: `src/index.css` sets `right: 1.25rem` without resetting `left: auto`, causing the floating card to occupy 74% of mobile screens and obscure the 3D book.
- **[P2] Undersized 10px Metadata & Low Contrast**: Sub-11px functional text and 2.5:1 contrast on `#a8a29e` fail WCAG standards.
- **[P2] Antiquarian Immersion Puncture**: Raw hex codes (`#172338`, `#e5be65`) displayed in bookbinding specifications.

#### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User)**: Cannot deep-link directly to a specific project via URL hash or use browser back/forward buttons.
- **Jordan (First-Timer)**: Hesitates on the 3D book; does not realize dragging spins it or clicking opens a monograph.
- **Morgan (Recruiter)**: Has 45 seconds between meetings; lacks an instant 1-click resume download or flat matrix overview.

#### Minor Observations

2. Index dropdown scrollbar progress tracks smoothly with the 3D carousel.
3. Preloader unmount delay can be tightened to eliminate lingering hidden DOM nodes.
